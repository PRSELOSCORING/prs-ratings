      How the ratings work

      • Elo-style rating: every shooter at a match is compared with every other shooter there. Beating higher-rated shooters earns more; losing to lower-rated shooters costs more.
      • Spread 133, 2 points per head-to-head, capped at +50 / −25 per match. New shooters start at 500.
      • Finishing order comes from Impact Scoring, including tiebreakers. Shooters with fewer than 50 impacts are left out of that match.
      • Mulligan: each shooter's season replayed without the one match that cost them the most points, against the same opponents at their real ratings. Needs 2+ matches this season; nobody else's rating changes.
      • Head-to-Head: the chance one shooter finishes ahead of the other, from the same formula the ratings use: 1 / (1 + 10(rating B − rating A) / 133). A 133-point edge is about 10 to 1.
      • Click a shooter to see their match-by-match history. Shooter ID is their Impact Scoring ID.
